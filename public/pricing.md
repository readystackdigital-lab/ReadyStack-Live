# Pricing: ReadyStack Digital

ReadyStack Digital builds and manages websites for small businesses across Australia, rebuilds slow or outdated ones, and deploys 24/7 AI receptionists that answer calls and enquiries. Every estimate is confirmed as a free, fixed quote within 24 hours.

Website rebuilds are priced the same as new builds, by page count.

## Website build (one-time, AUD)

- 1 page (landing page): $799–$1,199
- Up to 5 pages: $1,799–$2,499
- 6–10 pages: $2,799–$3,799
- 10+ pages or custom builds: priced after a short consultation

## Add-ons (one-time unless noted)

- Booking system: $299
- Blog (CMS setup): $399
- SEO setup: $449
- AI Agent: Chat: no setup fee with any website build, $99/month to run (answers questions about the business and its services on the website)
- AI Agent: Reception: $1,499 setup + $149/month (adds real phone answering, qualifying and calendar booking) [most popular]
- AI Agent: Custom: priced after a consultation (agents built for a specific job, e.g. chasing quiet quotes, review requests, rebooking reminders, appointment reminders, invoice follow-ups, multi-location routing)
- Online store (e-commerce): priced on consultation
- Business email (Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace): included in quote

## Ongoing website care (monthly, AUD, no lock-in contract)

- Managed Website Care: $99/month (hosting, domain, security certificate, enquiry forms tested, search visibility monitored)
- Website Care Plus: $149/month (Managed Care plus monthly blog posts and priority support)

## Example

A 5-page business website with a booking system and SEO setup: $2,547–$3,247 one-time, plus $99/month for Managed Website Care.
